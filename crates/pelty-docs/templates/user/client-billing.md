# Invoices and payments

{{nav:client:billing}}

## Pay an invoice

**Invoices** (`/invoices`) lists your invoices. Open one to see the lines, download the PDF (or a UBL e-invoice for accounting software), and choose **Pay**: you pick one of the payment methods your provider offers (card, PayPal, bank transfer and so on). Some methods send you to the payment provider and back.

- **Pay several at once**: select unpaid invoices and pay them together, or merge them into one invoice.
- **Use your credit**: apply account credit to an invoice.
- **Refunds** on an invoice are shown on it.

## Credit, saved cards and gift cards

**Credit & payment methods** (`/billing`):

- Add funds to your account credit; credit is used to pay invoices.
- Save a payment method and make one the default. If your provider uses automatic payments, due invoices are charged to the default method; you get an email when a charge fails.
- Redeem gift cards. To see a card's balance first without using it, open `/gift-card`.

## If you do not pay

Your provider sends payment reminders before and after the due date. Services of overdue invoices can be suspended, and are unsuspended when the invoice is paid.

# Ordering

## The store

**Store** (`/store`) lists the product groups and products your provider sells. Pick a billing cycle (for example monthly or annually), fill any options and add it to the cart. Domains are searched at `/domain-search` and added to the same cart.

At **Checkout** you review the cart, apply a coupon code and confirm your details. Placing the order creates an invoice; once it is paid the order is accepted (automatically or after a check by staff) and your services are set up.

## Quotes

Your provider can send you a quote instead. **Quotes** (`/quotes`) shows them: download the PDF, then accept or decline. Accepting turns the quote into an invoice.

## Affiliates

If your provider runs an affiliate programme, **Affiliates** (`/affiliate`) shows your referral link, your referrals and commissions, marketing materials and payouts.
